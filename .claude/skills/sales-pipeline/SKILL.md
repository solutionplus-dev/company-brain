# Pipeline

1. A stage changes only on evidence, with date and source.
2. Every open opportunity has one next step, one owner, one date. No date means it is not being worked.
3. An offer draft takes scope from the meeting notes and price from the owner. Never invent or discount a price.
4. Material under a confidentiality agreement: never list first.
5. Draft; people send.
