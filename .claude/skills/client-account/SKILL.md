# A client's files

1. New client: copy `01_Clients/_TEMPLATE/` to `01_Clients/<slug>/`, add the object to `CLIENTS.json` (billing entity `undecided` until the owner decides), add a watchlist row.
2. Ask first what data the client will send. Confidential or special-category: the never list and `.brainignore` before anything is filed.
3. `STATUS.md` "Now": what they asked, what we promised and where that is written, what is blocked. When "Now" passes about 25 lines, the oldest blocks move word for word to `STATUS_HISTORY.md`.
4. Before a call: the last three meetings, open actions for this client, invoices open, promises due, the open questions.
5. Nothing about another client goes into this folder.
