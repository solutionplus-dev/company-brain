# 03_Sales — rules for agents working on sales

Read the root `AGENTS.md` first; nothing here weakens it.

`PIPELINE_NOW.md` is the one page: every open opportunity with its stage, next step, owner and date. `Leads/<slug>/` holds what a prospect sent and what we sent them. `Templates/` holds offers and proposals.

1. **A stage changes on evidence** (a message, a meeting, a signature), with the date and the source.
2. **A price in an offer comes from the owner.** An agent drafts structure and scope; it does not set or discount a price.
3. **Material under a confidentiality agreement** goes on the never list before it is filed (root rule 8).
4. **Which legal entity makes the offer** is the owner's decision; `undecided` until it is made.
5. **Agents draft; people send.**

Weekly Part A item 3 reads the pipeline for work that can be invoiced.
