# The brain's own to-do list

What the brain itself still has to do to get better. Each weekday block of the daily run (`00_Brain/SCHEDULES/daily.md` Step 3b) takes the open items of its kind first, ticks an item only with the date and the file that proves it, and writes here what it could not finish. Monday's weekly run names every open item older than two weeks.

Nothing here needs the owner. What needs the owner's yes is a proposal in `IMPROVEMENTS.md`; what the owner has to do is on the root `TODO.md`.

Line shape: `- [ ] <added> · <kind: apply | evaluate | enrich | clean | audit | dream | improve> · <what, one step> · source: <file>`

Done lines move to the bottom section with the date and one line of what changed. Nothing is deleted.
