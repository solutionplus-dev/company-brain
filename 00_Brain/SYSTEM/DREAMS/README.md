# Dreams

Once a week the brain reads everything that happened and writes down what it noticed: `<yyyy>-W<ww>.md`, written by Saturday's block of the daily run (`00_Brain/SCHEDULES/daily.md` Step 3b; Monday's weekly run writes it when Saturday's did not finish, so the dream is never skipped).

A dream **proposes and never applies**. It has five buckets, at most three entries each, every entry with its evidence and the exact change it suggests:

1. **Notice** — a pattern across days that no single run reported.
2. **Knowledge** — a fact worth a topic note, or a note the week made wrong.
3. **Rules** — a rule applied two ways, overridden, or missing.
4. **Routines** — a checklist item to add, merge or drop; a new routine as a proposed file.
5. **For the owner** — an opportunity or a risk with a recommended next step.

The three strongest entries appear on Monday's `TODO.md` under "The brain suggests". The owner answers yes, no or a correction. Accepted rule and routine changes go through `../IMPROVEMENTS.md`.

Why keep the files: a dismissed idea that comes back three times is itself a finding.
