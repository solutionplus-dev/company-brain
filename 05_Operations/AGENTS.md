# 05_Operations — rules for agents working on operations

Read the root `AGENTS.md` first; nothing here weakens it.

`Team/` (roles, onboarding, who may see what), `Tools/` (every subscription: what it is for, which entity pays, renewal date, who owns the account), `Vendors/` (contracts and contacts by role).

1. **A subscription is an obligation.** A renewal date also goes to `finance/OBLIGATIONS.md`.
2. **Access is written down, secrets are not.** "Who can open the bank" belongs here; the password never does.
3. **A personal file found here is not opened**; it is listed for the owner to move.
